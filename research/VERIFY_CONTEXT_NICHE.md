## Verdict
- **The niche is real and healthy**, but "millions per video" is the outlier, not the norm. Typical established channel: **50k–250k per Short**; viral hits (1M–16M) roughly 1 in 20–30 posts.
- **Small channels can win:** ClipsTycoon (28k subs) gets a 39k median and has a 1.4M hit — views are not locked to big channels.
- **Narration matters:** narrated StreamFlix (59k median at 427k subs) vs plain-clip Kai Cenat Shorts (15.5k) — and the Oct-2026 originality rule pushes further in our favour.
- **Our start is normal:** first Short 2.5k views with 61% stayed = within the normal new-channel range (50–500 first-48h is typical; we beat it).
- **Money reality:** at ~60k median × 30 Shorts/month ≈ 1.8M views ≈ $50–300/month from Shorts. The money is in long-form (StreamFlix long-form median 588k, Internet Anarchist 599k) + campaigns.

## What this means for Context Loading
1. Keep going — the niche is correct, but judge success over 30+ posts, not single videos.
2. Targets: weeks 1–4 = 1k–10k per Short; months 2–3 = 10k–60k median with occasional 100k+ hits.
3. Start weekly long-form early (that's where StreamFlix-type channels make their money).
