# BEST YOUTUBE SHORTS NICHE — deep research report (Nov 2026)

## 0. Two facts that change everything (official YouTube, announced Aug 10 2026, effective Feb 1 2027)
1. **Shorts ad revenue only pays channels with ≥ 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** (~111k/day). Below that you keep long-form earnings only.
2. **New YPP entry doubles:** 1,000 subs + **8,000 long-form watch hours / 365 d** or **20M Shorts views / 90 d**. Shorts-feed watch time does **not** count toward the 8,000 hours. (CreatorDB: median channel ≈ 3,400 h/yr.)
→ For a new channel, Shorts are a **funnel**, not the paycheck. Profit = long-form watch time + campaigns + sponsors/affiliates. Pick a niche that has a natural long-form version.

## 2. Money per niche (vendor data, directional)
- Long-form RPM: Education & Science median **$10.22** (AIR), Finance $10–30+, true crime/horror $4–9, Entertainment ~$2.4, Gaming ~$2.05.
- Shorts RPM: $0.02–1.48 by niche (AIR 274 ch.) — irrelevant below 10M/90d after Feb 2027.
- TikTok Creator Rewards (> 60 s): finance ~$1.6, most niches $0.4–1.0 per 1k qualified views.
- Kids/"made for kids" CPM $1–4 (no personalised ads) → avoid.

## 3. Policy risk landscape
- Reused-content demonetization waves in 2026 (r/NewTubers reports), Oct 2026 Shorts ranking favours originality, Kapwing: 21% of new-user Shorts feed is AI slop → YouTube is purging templated/AI/reused formats.
- Highest risk: compilations of TV/sports/social clips with little narration, AI-voiced templated channels, Reddit text narration, reused speeches.
- Safest: commentary/narration with a recognisable identity, original scripts, footage the creator authorises (campaigns), long-form with real structure.

## 5. Verdict
**Winner: stay in streamer stories — but evolve Context Loading into "internet & streamer culture, explained".**
- It is the only niche where we have a structural edge: free legal-grey→authorised footage access (Kick/Twitch + paid campaigns), tools already built, proven early result (Speed short 61% stayed on a new channel).
- Low concentration (top channel holds 8% of results) = room for a new brand; ceiling proven (StreamFlix 16M Short, 588k median long-form; Internet Anarchist 1.87M subs, 599k median long-form).
- It has the long-form version the 2027 rules demand: weekly 12–20 min narrated listicles/explainers ("15 Times Streamers Saved Lives", "The Rise of X") — 8,000 watch hours ≈ 50k long-form views at 10 min avg view time.
- Double income: the same research feeds clipping campaigns (paid per view today, no YPP needed).

**Runner-up (2nd channel later):** History/"explained" with public-domain/archival visuals (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, NASA) + our narrator → highest RPM long-form we can produce legally. Start only after Context Loading has 30+ posts.

**Avoid:** satisfying, motivation, soccer edits, Reddit TTS, raw animal clips — high views but the 2026 enforcement trend is aimed squarely at them.
