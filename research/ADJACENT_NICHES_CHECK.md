independent creator channels only (media brands removed). 2 discovery rounds, 24 search queries, 36 channels measured.

## Verdict
1. **No similar niche is consistently "way better."** In every niche most small channels sit at hundreds–low thousands; winners are
   individual channels with strong execution, not whole niches.
2. **Two exceptions:**
   - **Street interviews** — highest ceilings measured, but requires filming strangers in public on camera (different business).
   - **Rapper / hip-hop stories** — one channel (Cage Culture) performs ~3–4× our niche's ratio; the bigger channel (Cilvanis) doesn't.
     Promising but unproven (n=3). Audience overlaps ours (Kai/Speed collabs with rappers).
3. Streamer stories are **as good as or better than** the alternatives on typical views, and better for us on feasibility
   (Kick/Twitch access, tools built, campaigns pay on the same footage).

## Recommendation
Stay with Context Loading. Test hip-hop/rapper-on-stream stories as ~20% of uploads (e.g., rappers on Kai/Adin streams) and
compare their 72-h views against pure streamer stories after 10 posts each. Revisit street interviews only if you want to film.

## Limitations
Small samples per niche (1–10 channels); search-discovered channels skew small; view counts are a snapshot.
