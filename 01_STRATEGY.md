# 01 — STRATEGY: how a daily-posting channel actually makes money (Nov 2026)

## The money math (know this cold)
| Source | Pays roughly | 1M views ≈ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube **Shorts** ad share (YPP) | $0.03–0.30 per 1k engaged views (US highest, ~$0.33; finance ≈ top, entertainment/gaming low) | **$30–300** | Pool-based, varies monthly. Needs YPP (1k subs + 10M Shorts views/90d or 4k watch hours/12mo) |
| YouTube **long-form** ads | $2–8 RPM typical (finance $10–30+, gaming $1–4) | $2,000–8,000 | 10–50× Shorts per view. Needs watch time |
| TikTok **Creator Rewards** | ~$0.40–1.00 per 1k *qualified* views | $400–1,000 | Only videos **> 60 s**, original, 10k followers, 100k views/30d, 18+, eligible country (Sweden may NOT be eligible — check in app) |
| Instagram Reels | No reliable per-view pay (bonuses come and go) | ~$0 | Use for reach, brand deals, campaign views |
| **Clipping campaigns** (Whop Content Rewards etc.) | $0.05–3 per 1k verified views, median ≈ $1 | $200–3,000 | Pays on views from day 1, no YPP needed. Caps per clip, approval required |
| Sponsors / affiliates / UGC | per deal | — | Comes after an audience exists |

**Conclusion:** For a clip channel, *Shorts ad revenue alone is weak*. The real money paths are
(1) clipping campaigns now, (2) long-form watch time later, (3) sponsors/affiliates once niche audience exists.
Typical clipper income: beginners $100–500/mo, consistent daily multi-campaign clippers $1–3k/mo (vendor-reported).

## Run two tracks in parallel
### Track A — Cash now: clipping campaigns (daily)
- Join 2–4 campaigns with **English, clean, high-view creators** and a CPM ≥ $0.50 (or a good per-clip cap).
- Post each clip once per platform: TikTok + YouTube Shorts + IG Reels → 3 submissions per clip.
- Target: 2–3 clips/day total. Each clip must be a *different moment* (no duplicates — campaigns reject "spam").
- Prioritise campaigns that pay from low thresholds and allow all three platforms.

### Track B — Asset: one owned niche channel that is *original*
- Shorts-only clip reuploads are now downranked on YouTube (Oct 2026 originality update) and pay pennies.
- So the owned channel must add value: **narrated storytelling / analysis / rankings with your own script and voice (or text-led storytelling), original structure**.
- Formats that are original enough and scale daily:
  1. "The story of ___ in 45 seconds" (streamer drama / internet history), own script + voice/text, clips as B-roll.
  2. Ranked countdowns with commentary ("Top 5 ___ moments this week" + your verdict each step).
  3. "Explained" shorts: a viral moment + context the viewer didn't know.
- Then **long-form** (8–15 min) compilations/recaps of the same niche weekly — that's where RPM lives.
  Shorts feed long-form only when the topic is the same (AIR data: Shorts+long-form channels have more subs in most niches, but gaming is the exception — keep Shorts topically identical to long-form).

## Cadence (evidence-based)
- Metricool 2026 study (800k videos): best measured ROI at **2–4 Shorts/week & 2–4 long-form/week**; beyond that, diminishing returns.
- Daily posting is fine *if every post is original and passes the checklist*. 10+/day risks spam signals.
- Our cadence: Track A 2–3/day across campaign accounts; Track B 1 Short/day + 1 long-form/week.
- Commit to a format for **~30 posts** before judging it (see 05_TEST_PLAN).

## 90-day plan
| Weeks | Goal | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Prove the edit | 2 campaign clips/day. Track viewed-vs-swiped per clip. Fix hooks until ≥ 60% "stayed" |
| 3–4 | Pick winners | Keep the 2 best campaigns; drop campaigns where clips stall < 1k views |
| 5–8 | Build Track B | Launch owned niche channel: 1 narrated Short/day, same niche as campaigns where possible |
| 9–12 | Long-form | Weekly 8–12 min recap/compilation with commentary; aim for YPP watch hours |
| ongoing | Scale | Automate scouting with a daily scheduled Claude task reading this repo |

## What NOT to do
- Don't buy views/bots or use engagement pods (campaigns ban, platforms downrank).
- Don't repost raw clips or other clippers' edits.
- Don't post the same file twice on one platform, don't delete-and-reupload flops (looks spammy).
- Don't chase finance/AI-faceless trends without real originality — "inauthentic content" (mass-produced templated uploads) can demonetize the **whole channel**.
