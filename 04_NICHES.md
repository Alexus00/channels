# 04 — NICHES

## The trade-off
| Niche | Views | Pay/RPM | Fit for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streamer clips (Kick/Twitch IRL, chat roulette, reactions) | Very high | Shorts RPM tiny; long-form gaming $1–4 | **Great for campaigns (Track A)**, weak for ad revenue |
| Streamer drama / internet stories *narrated* | High | Entertainment long-form ~$2–4 | **Best owned-channel bet (Track B)**: original, daily-able, feeds long-form |
| Personal finance / investing / business | Medium | Highest ($10–30+ long-form; best Shorts RPM) | Needs real expertise; high competition |
| Tech / AI tools / software | Medium-high | $8–40 | Good if you can make tutorials/reviews |
| Horror / true crime / mysteries (narrated) | Very high (0.5–5M views common) | $4–8 | Strong storytelling niche, needs scripting |
| Fitness | Medium | Mid | Shorts+long-form combo works well (AIR: +41% subs) |
| Gaming highlights | High | Low | Shorts can *hurt* long-form here (AIR: long-form-only gaming channels have 85% more subs) |

(RPM figures are vendor estimates — directional only.)

## Recommendation
1. **Track A (now):** streamer campaigns — Gattouz0 (English only), JasonTheWeen, Danesh Diamonds, Preme, Greg LaVecchia when footage is available. Choose campaigns by: CPM × likely views × rule difficulty.
2. **Track B (owned):** "Streamer Stories" style channel — narrated 30–45 s stories about viral streamer moments (who, what happened, why it's crazy), using clips as B-roll + own text/voice. Weekly 10-min long-form "This week in streaming". Same niche as Track A so research is reused.
3. Test an alternative owned niche only after 30 posts of the first one (see 05).

## How to evaluate a new niche fast
- Search the niche on YouTube Shorts/TikTok: are 3+ small channels (<10k subs) getting 100k+ views? → demand exists for newcomers.
- Can you produce 1/day sustainably with the tools here? If not, skip.
- Is there a long-form version people watch? (needed for real money)
