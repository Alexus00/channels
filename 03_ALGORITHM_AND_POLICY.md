# 03 — ALGORITHM & POLICY (2026)

## What the feeds reward
**YouTube Shorts**
1. **Viewed vs. swiped away** = the hook gate. If weak, nothing else matters. Aim ≥ 65–70% "stayed" (YT Studio → Engagement → "Vad tittarna gjorde" in Swedish UI). Galloway-attributed data: best Shorts sat at 70–90%.
2. **Engaged views** (watched past the first seconds) drive promotion and pay — not raw plays.
3. **Completion / average % viewed**: >60% of viewers reaching the end strongly predicts success (Galloway, 3.3B views). Loops and short length push this >100%.
4. New channels get small test batches (50–300 viewers). Views going flat after ~3 h = failed the test batch → fix the hook on the next post, don't delete.

**TikTok**: completion, rewatches, shares, comments; the first 0.5–2 s decide. Creator Rewards only counts originals > 60 s.
**Instagram Reels**: watch time + sends (DM shares) dominate; Instagram restricted recommendations for accounts built mostly on others' content (Apr 2026).

## Originality rules — the big 2026 shift
- **YouTube, Oct 1 2026:** Shorts recommendations now *prioritise original content and reduce reach of re-uploads* from other creators. Ranking change, separate from monetization. What counts as original: **commentary, analysis, storytelling, or genuinely creative edits** — "add something viewers wouldn't get from the source alone". Permission/licensing does NOT make it original.
- **YouTube reused-content policy (monetization)**: clips/compilations need meaningful transformation to be in YPP. Applies channel-wide.
- **YouTube "inauthentic content"** (renamed July 15 2025): mass-produced, templated, repetitive uploads → can demonetize the whole channel. Avoid identical template spam; vary formats, add real commentary/script.
- **TikTok**: unoriginal/reposted clips (e.g. other watermarks, no edits) are ineligible for the For You feed (not deleted, just hidden).
- Campaign clips are the creator's *authorized* footage, but the platform originality ranking still applies → our edit must visibly add value (hook restructure, captions, zooms, cutaways, narrative, title card). For owned channels go further: own script/voice/analysis.

## Practical originality checklist
- [ ] Restructured (cold open, reordered beats) — not a straight cut of one clip
- [ ] Our captions + header + progress bar + zooms (visible editing layer)
- [ ] Our angle in the title/header (what's interesting about it)
- [ ] For owned channel: narration or commentary text that adds context/opinion
- [ ] No other creator's/clipper's watermark; never reupload another clipper's edit

## Copyright / fair use (not legal advice)
- Content ID is automated; it can claim even fair use. Disputes are manual.
- Music is the #1 risk in streams — cut or mute copyrighted songs playing in background where possible.
- Clipping campaigns = explicit permission from the creator for their footage (but not for third-party music/video they played).
- Reaction/commentary can be fair use (Klein v. Denims-type cases), but platform penalties can happen regardless.

## Monetization thresholds (verify in app — they change)
- YPP full: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/12 mo **or** 10M qualified Shorts views/90 days. Lower fan-funding tier (~500 subs) in some regions.
- TikTok Creator Rewards: 10k followers, 100k views/30 d, 18+, eligible region, videos > 1 min.
- Instagram: no dependable per-view pay; check Professional Dashboard.
